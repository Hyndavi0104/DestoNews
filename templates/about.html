About DestoNews

DestoNews is a modern and intelligent news platform designed to deliver the latest headlines from around the world based on your selected location.

Built using Django and the GNews API, the platform allows users to quickly browse trending news categories, save time, and stay informed effortlessly.

Why DestoNews?

  • Fast and reliable news fetching
  • Location-based trending headlines
  • Simple, clean, and user-friendly interface
  • Powered by Django authentication
